Star names

A CSV table linking star names and multilingual translations to source identifiers and catalog designations, including Bayer, Flamsteed, HD and Hipparcos fields where supplied. Derived from d3-celestial without geometry; it is independent of the magnitude-limited position subsets.

4869 rows · name table

CSV · 532.9 kB

This CSV contains names and identifiers without geometry. Use the identifiers to relate names to the relevant catalogs, checking their catalog conventions first.

Derived from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn and processed with R and sf by Diego Hernangómez. Distributed under the BSD 3-Clause license.

The DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7561601 identifies the Celestial Data collection. Downloads here follow the repository's main branch and may differ from an archived release.
